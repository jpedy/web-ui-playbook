# Legal notes — Iran trademark, copyright, prices ⏱

**Not legal advice.** Everything here is general information gathered from the Version 1.2 research report and its cited sources (as of 1404–1405 / 2025–2026). Rules, portals and fees change: **verify with a licensed Iranian IP attorney (وکیل ثبت برند) before filing or relying on any point.** The skill has not independently verified the fees or procedures.

## 1. What to tell the user, every time
- A logo is useful from day one, but **ownership comes from trademark registration**, not from making the file.
- Search for similar marks **before** printing anything (§2).
- If the logo contains Latin letters, also keep the **Persian-only** file (§3).

## 2. Similarity search before use
1. Reverse image search of `png/icon-512.png` (Google Lens, Yandex Images, TinEye) and of the main lockup.
2. Iran: the trademark database on the Intellectual Property Center portal (`iripo.ssaa.ir` / `ipm.ssaa.ir` → میز خدمات الکترونیک → ثبت علائم → جستجو در بانک اطلاعاتی), searching the name in the relevant Nice classes; and recent trademark notices in the official gazette (`rrk.ir` → آگهی‌های قانونی → علامت تجاری) during their 30-day objection window.
3. International: WIPO Global Brand Database (figurative codes, e.g. Vienna class 26 for geometric shapes), USPTO, EUIPO.

If something close turns up, change the mark — do not argue that it is "different enough".

## 3. Registration in Iran (as described in the report — verify)
- Filing is online through the portal above; the applicant needs a judiciary **Sana (ثنا)** account, a digital signature certificate, and a business permit matching the class, plus a logo image (max 10 × 10 cm).
- **Latin letters:** the report states that a mark containing Latin letters or non-Persian words requires a **commercial card (کارت بازرگانی)**. Many small businesses do not have one — that is why the kit always includes `svg/logo-fa-only.svg` (Persian only, zero Latin letters). Confirm the current rule with an IP attorney before filing.
- Examination plus two gazette publications: about 45–75 working days, including a 30-day objection period; protection lasts 10 years and can be a base for an international (Madrid) filing.
- Marks are refused if they contain national flags, state or military emblems, the Red Crescent, sacred names or words used commercially, or content against public morality. The recipes avoid all of these; never add them.
- Use an IP attorney for several classes, after a refusal, or to file an objection.

## 4. Copyright and AI-made logos (US-centered; other countries differ)
- In the United States, the Copyright Office's position — upheld in *Thaler v. Perlmutter* (D.C. Cir., 2025) — is that material generated by AI **without human creative control** cannot be copyrighted; prompting alone is not enough. Human selection, arrangement and editing can be protected.
- A logo built by this skill is assembled from geometric recipes and a font's outlines; its copyright status is uncertain and depends on how much the user shapes it. **Trademark protection does not depend on copyright** — a distinctive mark used in commerce can be registered.
- Iranian copyright law's treatment of AI output is not covered by the sources — ask an attorney.
- Fonts: the bundled OFL fonts allow outlining into a logo (assets/fonts/FONTS.md). Commercial fonts need a license that covers logos.
- Open-source icons (Lucide, Heroicons, Tabler…) may be used in a UI but **not as the logo**: not distinctive, not ownable.

## 5. When to hire a designer — price ranges ⏱ (report, 1403–1405 / 2025–2026; verify before quoting)
| Option | Range |
|---|---|
| Freelance marketplaces in Iran (Ponisha, Karlancer, Parscoders) — beginner / experienced | about 0.5–3 million / 5–15 million Toman |
| Iranian Graphic Designers Society (IGDS) tariff — logo or logotype with a brand manual | 45 million Toman (studios often price 10–20% below) |
| Self-filed trademark registration (fees, one class) / through an agency | about 3 million / 6–9.5 million Toman |
| International: budget marketplaces / senior freelancer / studio | $100–600 / $1,500–5,000 / $10,000+ |

Recommend a human designer for calligraphic logotypes, mascots, large rebrands and packaging (logo-fundamentals.md §6).

## Common mistakes
- Presenting these notes as legal certainty.
- Skipping the similarity search because the logo was "generated from scratch".
- Delivering only a bilingual logo to a business without a commercial card.
